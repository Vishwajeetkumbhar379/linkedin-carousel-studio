# How AI *edited* this video: concept and beat sheet (24 Oct 2026, for approval)

Script: `content/shoot-sheets/2026-10-explainers.json`, sheet HOW-AI-EDIT. 11 lines, 141 words, about 67 s.
Times below are **stand-in** word timings; Vish's real voiceover re-times everything (every cue is `cue("phrase")`).
Pictures: a fresh brainstorm agent wrote 5 to 7 ideas per line; a second fresh agent picked one per line for clarity with the sound off, surprise and spectacle.

## The idea: the world is the reel's own timeline
There are only two worlds: **his room** and **the timeline of this video**.
- Line 1 freezes his face and shatters it into the 39 clips, which drop into his phone's camera roll.
- Line 2 goes in through the phone: Instagram's progress bar becomes a rail, the playhead becomes a cart.
- Lines 3 to 9 are stations along the rail, each made of its own lane's material: the audio lane (words, flags, the cut), the caption lane (split-flap tiles), the video lane (frames and mattes), the render (browser frames, the wall of 1,800), the music lane (a punched hole).
- Line 10 leaves through draft 12's frame back into his room. Line 11 stops at the end of the bar, where "you" are.

Every crossing goes through a frame, so no change of world is a jump. Callbacks carry the film: the 39 clips (1, 2), the word flags (3, 5), the shutter click and the 1,800 wall (7, 8), silence held back until 9, RISH to VISH (4, 10).

## Colour means one thing each
- **Amber #E7A15A:** what to follow (the piece being worked on, the playhead, the cut, the caption pill).
- **Cool lane blue:** structure (lanes, rails, ticks). Never the subject.
- **White:** his words and real data (timestamps, durations, counts).
- **Red:** wrong, used once: every "RISH" in line 4.

## What Vish films (phone in hand, night-studio light)

## Style frames of beat 1 (same moment in each)
- **A, luminous 3D (recommended):** the timeline world at dusk: dark lanes to the horizon with clips lying on them, his frame as a lit screen cracking with amber light, shards as glowing glass. It is the world lines 2 to 9 live in, so beat 1 already sets it up. Closest to Charles's grammar.
- **B, realistic 3D:** his real desk at night under one lamp, mug and notebook, shards as glass with shadows. Warm and tactile, but the timeline world of lines 2 to 9 would be a separate place.
- **C, motion graphics:** flat and exact on a night canvas: hairline cracks, shards flipping on clean curves into a flat phone, a mono counter. Clearest and cheapest, least awe.
