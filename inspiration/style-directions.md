# Style directions

All five keep the locked rules (handle top-left, pill counter, tinted cards, #7F77DD, 0.5px borders, badge, save CTA). They differ in ground, type voice, how much 3D carries the slide, and motion. A, B and C have rendered samples in `out/`.

## A · Studio Light (sample: EU watermark carousel)
**Evolution, not replacement.** Paper #FBFAF8 with a faint violet wash, Geist type, tinted cards exactly as today. 3D glass and clay heroes on the cover and section breaks; Dot the mascot in the corner of content slides.
- From: the locked carousel system + Build with Vish day mode + taste-skill restraint.
- Why it could work: instantly recognisable as "Vish's carousels", premium without being loud, the safest bet for saves.
- Risk: calm can read as quiet in a busy feed; the cover hero has to do the stopping.

## B · Night Lab (sample: ChatGPT image-ads video)
Near-black #08070D, iris violet #A99CFF, aurora teal, warm sol for numbers. Kinetic word-by-word captions, slow camera drift over floating 3D objects, starfield depth.
- From: Build with Vish's cinematic homepage + the vish-linkedin-content skill's dark motion style.
- Why it could work: dark covers stand out on LinkedIn's light feed; video is where dwell time grows fastest.
- Risk: dark slides are harder to read for long text; keep it for video and covers.

## C · Field Notes (sample: Meta creator hub single image)
Bone paper #ECE7DC, deep teal ink, Syne display headlines, Public Sans body. Editorial and confident, with clay objects in Vish's colours.
- From: the portfolio site (the "hire me" voice).
- Why it could work: looks like nobody else in AI x marketing (which is mostly purple-on-white or black); reads as an operator, not a tool reviewer. Strong for opinion and career posts.
- Risk: drifts from the violet identity; taste-skill flags warm-paper palettes as an AI default, so it must stay disciplined (teal ink, violet accent only).

## D · Clay Diorama (not sampled)
Every slide is a small full-bleed 3D scene (clay desk, glass cards floating in it); text sits on frosted panels inside the scene.
- Why: maximum scroll-stop and "3D carousel" feel.
- Risk: slow to render, text competes with the scene, easy to tip into generic 2026 "clay everything".

## E · Notebook (not sampled)
Text-first, almost no 3D: big Geist numbers, hairline rules, one mascot sticker. For rapid same-day news posts.
- Why: fastest to produce, highest legibility, good for a "breaking" lane.
- Risk: closest to what everyone else does.

## My recommendation
**A as the default carousel system, B for video and dark covers, C reserved for opinion and career posts.** One identity, three registers, all from tokens.json.
