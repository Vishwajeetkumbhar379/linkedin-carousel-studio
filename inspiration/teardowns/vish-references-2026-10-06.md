## Rules this added to the system
- Grain on every slide (7 to 10%), never a flat fill.
- Light comes from 2 or 3 blurred sources, and they continue across slide edges (seamless swipe).
- Titles: Geist 600, centred, about 90 px. No heavy 700-800 headlines.
- One hero object per slide at most, plus optional depth-of-field echo.
- Chrome in four corners only; the middle belongs to the idea.
