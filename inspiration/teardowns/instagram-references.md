## Patterns (no copying of layout, art or text)

1. **The keyword-comment gate drives the comment count.** The two biggest posts have more comments than likes. Each comment is a one-word reply. On Instagram this works. 2. **Numbered "build these" lists win saves.** avani.codes: 12 concrete projects, each with a name a beginner can picture. → Our list slides name concrete things (contract clause, report, prompt), never abstractions.
3. **Tool + outcome in the hook.** "Claude + WhatsApp", "$7k/month YouTube channel with Claude". → Name the tool and the result, but never a money claim we can't prove.
4. **Partnership disclosure is visible** (#higgsfieldpartner). → Any sponsored post gets "Werbung/Anzeige"-level clarity, per Vish's own ad-labels carousel.
