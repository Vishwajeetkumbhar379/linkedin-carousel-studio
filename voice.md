# Voice: Vish
Plain, direct, warm. Short sentences, one person ("you"). British English spelling. No em or en dashes. Lead with the useful thing.
First person for opinions ("I'd start with...", "What I check first..."). Insider calm, not hype. Explain any technical word in a few words.
Banned: delve, game-changer, unlock, unleash, supercharge, seamless, leverage (verb), robust, elevate, harness, empower, revolutionise, cutting-edge, landscape, realm, "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in". Run SlopMonster on every script, caption and reply.

## Vish's spoken voice (from his own voice notes, 21 to 22 Oct 2026)

- Avoid in his scripts: aphorisms and slogans ("Steal whatever works"), tidy triplets in every line, rhetorical one-word sentences in a row, anything he wouldn't say to a friend on a call.
- Keep: concrete facts (27, a year ago, 2.5 years, five years, February) and one honest feeling per beat.
