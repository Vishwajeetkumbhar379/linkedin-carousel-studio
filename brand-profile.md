# Brand profile: Vish Kumbhar (Build with Vish)
Locked 14 Oct 2026 from Vish's picks in the playbook (https://claude.ai/artifact/EKQM5bY8XGoZskinThRxNz).

## Positioning
"I signed 850+ creator deals on the brand side. Now I show marketers and creators how to do that work with AI."
Audience: marketers, founders and creators who work with creators or want brand deals. Goal: reach, then freelance and brand-deal work paid in EUR, USD or CHF.

## Proof (the only facts about Vish we may use)
- Years of brand-side influencer marketing and brand management; 850+ creator deals; led teams. No brand or employer names.
- Moved to Germany; doing an MBA; job-hunting in Germany with an AI job-search agent he built.
- Builds AI tools and workflows as a non-engineer (Claude Projects, Skills, connectors; this content engine; buildwithvish.netlify.app).
- Never invent results, numbers, quotes or client stories.

## Pillars (mix)
P1 New AI drops, shown working (30%, Format B demo) · P2 AI employees for marketing (25%) · P3 Brand-side secrets (20%, Format A split) · P4 Content frameworks with AI (15%, Format A) · P5 Build in public (10%).

## Signature series
Hire This AI · Brand-Side · New Drop · Steal My Workflow · Ship Log.

## Formats (studied from @kallawaymarketing, @justyn.ai, @leadgenman; analyses in research/creators/)
- Format A, split explainer (Kallaway): Vish cut out in the lower half; animated cards, diagrams and real examples above; headline on top; word-by-word captions; a cut every 2 to 3 s; punch-in (~115%) on the key line of each beat; whoosh on card changes, pop on reveals, quiet ambient bed; named framework + step count + one surprising comparison; re-hook every 15 to 20 s; comment keyword early and at the end.
- Format B, tool demo (Justyn): proof first in 2 s; real screen recordings of the tool; Vish in a cut-out bubble; 2 to 3 giant words on punchlines; at most two camera spots.
- One edit, two vertical layouts from layers (never crops): 9:16 (Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok) and 4:5 (LinkedIn, IG feed). No 16:9 or long-form for now.

## Look
Night studio: dark wall, one warm lamp beside Vish. Tokens: night #14161B, lamp #E7A15A, accent #3049E0 (current caption word, highlights), paper #F4F5F7. Captions in a heavy condensed sans, uppercase for punch words.

## Rhythm
3 talking videos a week (one filming session), plus guide carousels between. Vish films on his phone with a mic (filming checklist in the playbook).

## Hard nos
AI voices, AI avatars, generated faces, stock footage that doesn't match the line, fake numbers, automated likes or comments on other people's posts.
