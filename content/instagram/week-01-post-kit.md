# Week 1 intro reel: posting kit

Upload `video-projects/week-01-intro/out/week1-intro-9x16.mp4` (100 s, 9:16). The file holds Vish's voice and the sound
design only. The music is added inside Instagram, so it is licensed there and the reel joins each song's audio page
(that page is where trending-audio reach comes from). A song baked into the file is usually muted or blocked, and it
does not count toward the trend.

## Music (Instagram editor → audio → "Add to mix", up to 20 tracks per reel)
| Track | Song | Where | Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Someday We Will Dream About Today**, The Sways | 0:00 → 1:32.6 (until he starts dancing) | music ~20 %, original audio 100 % |
| 2 | **The cat song** ("I got that feeling…") from the @meowtakeover cat reel | 1:32.6 → 1:40.2 (the dance and the cats) | ~70 % |

- Track 2 setup: open the original cat reel, tap its audio name, then **Save**. In your reel: Add to mix → Saved, then drag it to start at 1:32.6.
- Why track 1: it's one of the most-used trending sounds right now (213K reels per Scott Social's 19 Oct 2026 list). It's soft and uplifting, and it's used for "new beginning" moments. It sits under talking without fighting it, and the title fits a Week 1 of 52 story.
- Alternative with more energy: **What You Waiting For?** by Gwen Stefani (111K reels, the "Take a chance cause you might grow" trend). Its trending section has a cheeky lyric, so listen first.
- Both songs are marked **not available for Business accounts**. If they don't show up, switch to a Creator account: Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to creator account.
- Check before posting: if a song's Reels count has dropped in the app, pick the top rising sound in Professional dashboard → Trends → Audio.
