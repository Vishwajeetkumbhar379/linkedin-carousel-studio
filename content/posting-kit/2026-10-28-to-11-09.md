### Sun 1 Nov · Carousel: write like you, not like AI (HUMAN)
- **Files:** `out/week03/carousel-human/slides.zip`.
```
Everyone can tell you used AI.

"Unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "in today's fast-paced world". The fix isn't a better tool. It's a picture of your voice:

1. Feed it five things you wrote that sound like you, and ask for a voice card
2. Add a ban list: the words that make you wince
3. Make it grade its own draft against the card and rewrite the weakest line
4. Read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it to a person, cut it
```
