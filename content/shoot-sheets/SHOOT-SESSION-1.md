# Shoot session 1 (about 4 h): one session gives 35 to 45 posts

**Before you start:**
- Clip-on mic (Lightning), a plain wall behind you for the cut-out, the lamp beside you, phone at 4K 30 fps.
- Clap at the start of each block.
- Fluffed a line? Pause and say it again. The edit keeps the last take.
- Change your top between blocks.

## 1. Clip pack (15 min, warm-up). Reused in every edit.
Say each one 2 or 3 times with different energy.
- **Openers:**
  - "Hey, I'm Vish."
  - "I'm 27. 220 companies said no. So I'm building my own job."
  - "Week 3 of 52." Then the same line for weeks 4 to 10, one take each.
- **Bridges:**
  - "But here's the thing."
  - "Real talk."
  - "Hot take."
  - "Let me show you."
  - "Here's what nobody tells you."
  - "And that's when it hit me."
  - "Trust me on this one."
  - "Okay, I tested it."
- **Endings:**
  - "Comment the word on screen and I'll send it to you."
  - "Follow to see if I make it."
  - "Tell me I'm wrong."
  - "Save this one."
  - "See you next week."
- **Silent reactions (3 s each, eyes on the lens):** laugh, smirk, shocked, "No.", "Wait... what?", "Ouch.", "Okay, that's actually smart.", slow head shake, slow nod, eye-roll, facepalm, thinking face.
- **Moves:** walk into frame and sit; lean into the lens; close the laptop and look up.

## 4. Story interview (90 min). 12 to 15 story reels.
Talk like a podcast. Long answers are good. Tell real scenes: where you were, what you saw, what you said. Skip any question you don't want.
1. The day you decided to quit your job. Where were you? What did you tell yourself?
2. Your first week in Germany. What surprised you, and what hurt?
3. Rejection number 1 versus rejection number 220. What changed in you?
4. The worst rejection you got. Read it or describe it.
5. When did the debt start to scare you? What's the 40k from? (Currency on screen.)
6. The lowest night so far. Where, what time, what were you thinking?
7. A moment where not speaking German really cost you.
8. "Do everything right and you'll be fine." When did you stop believing it?
9. Why you stopped applying and started building in public.
10. Why exactly 3,000 € a month? What changes when you hit it?
11. What scares you most about putting your life online?
12. What happens if you fail at week 52?
13. The first creator deal you ever signed. What went wrong, or right? (No brand names.)
14. The craziest thing a creator asked for in a negotiation. (No names. True only.)
15. A campaign that flopped and what it taught you.
16. What brands say about creators when creators aren't in the room.
17. The biggest mistake creators make when they DM a brand.
18. The moment AI clicked for you.
19. What you built with Claude that you're proudest of. Show it on screen.
20. What you'd tell a marketer who's scared of AI.
21. Why Sri Lanka, why now, with 40k of debt?
22. What does a "made it" day look like?
23. What you'd tell yourself one year ago.
24. One thing you're embarrassed to admit.
25. What you want people to say about you at week 52.

## 6. "I tried it" tests (10 min today, then 20 s a day for a week). 3 series.
Film today: the opener "Everyone says X. I'm testing it for 7 days." Then a 20-second selfie check-in every evening with the real numbers.
- **T1, from 3 Nov: AI applies to jobs for me for 7 days.** Applications, replies, interviews.
- **T2: Claude runs my Instagram for 7 days.** Ideas, captions, replies drafted by Claude; I post. Followers and comments.
- **T3: the viral "$10k a month AI side hustle", tested for 5 days.** Hours spent, money made.

## 7. B-roll (20 min). 5 s each, steady, portrait.
- **Work:** typing; scrolling job ads; a rejection email (blurred); the bank app (blurred); a notebook with the plan.
- **Home:** cooking; a coffee being made; rain on the window; the laptop at night; the calendar.
- **Outside:** German streets; the train; groceries; a walk.
- **Sri Lanka prep:** packing, and the flight confirmation (number hidden).
