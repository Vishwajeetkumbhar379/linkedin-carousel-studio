# Shoot day 1: 35 videos in one day

Every script is on the teleprompter page (Shoot Sheets), one line at a time with delivery marks. This page gives the order to film them in, so you set up each spot once.

- **Total:** about 5 hours of filming plus breaks. Most scripts take 2 to 4 minutes with retakes.
- **Retakes:** if you fluff a line, pause, then say the whole line again. The edit keeps the last take.
- **Outfits:** change your top between blocks. Then the posts don't look like they were filmed on the same day.

## Before you start (10 min)
- [ ] Phone at 4K 30 fps, Cinematic mode off, lens cleaned. The front camera is fine for blocks C and D.
- [ ] Mic: if you still have none, sit close to the phone in the smallest, softest room you have. Curtains and a bed kill echo. Bare walls make it.
- [ ] Turn off the fridge, fan and washing machine, and close the window. The Voice Isolator fixes a lot, but not a room that rings.
- [ ] Light: face the window, or put the lamp beside you at head height (the night-studio look).
- [ ] Clap once at the start of every take. It marks the take for the edit.

## B-roll to grab while you're set up (20 min)
Typing a DM, scrolling a creator grid, signing or highlighting a printed page, a calculator on your phone, packing a bag, your street, a language app, the laptop by the window, a coffee being made. Shoot 5 seconds of each, steady, in both landscape and portrait.

## After filming
Upload everything to the Drive folder in one go (originals, not screen recordings of the clips). The edit pipeline takes it from there. Each topic gets one reel, a cover, and a carousel where it fits.
