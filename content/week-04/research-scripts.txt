THE 5 RULES EVERY SCRIPT NOW PASSES
1. Hook: topic in the first 5 words, then a turn the other way. A statement, never a question.
2. Lock-in: the 2 lines after the hook prove it's worth staying (your real proof, like 850+ deals).
3. Stakes, a big question the viewer guesses at, a twist that makes sense after, and a rehook by 20 s. Beats joined with 'but' or 'so', never 'and then'.
4. Say who it's for in line 1 or 2, and promise a small number ('3 lines', '2 flights').
5. One fun beat and one line people repeat to a friend. One CTA only.
