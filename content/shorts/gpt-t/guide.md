# The T in GPT: the paper, the eight people, where they went

The one paper behind ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, in 3 minutes, with links to the originals.
Sources linked under every point, checked 23 Oct 2026 · @buildwithvish · October 2026

## 1. What GPT stands for
Generative Pre-trained Transformer. OpenAI's 2018 paper named the approach: generative pre-training on top of a Transformer.
Source: [OpenAI, 2018](https://cdn.openai.com/research-covers/language-unsupervised/language_understanding_paper.pdf)

## 2. The paper
"Attention Is All You Need" (June 2017), written by eight researchers at Google. It introduced the Transformer, the T.
Source: [arXiv 1706.03762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)

## 3. Why it mattered
Older models read text one word after another. The Transformer looks at every word at once and learns which words matter to each other ("attention"). That made training on huge amounts of text practical.

## 4. The patent
Google holds US patent 10,452,978, "Attention-based sequence transduction neural networks" (granted 22 Oct 2019), yet the paper was public and the idea was used across the industry.
Source: [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10452978B2)

## 5. Where the eight went
All eight co-authors have now left Google, most to found or join other AI companies.
Source: [36Kr, June 2026](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3872257909068806)

## 6. The $2.7 billion return
Google brought Noam Shazeer back through a roughly $2.7 billion Character.AI deal in 2024. In June 2026 he joined OpenAI.
Source: [Axios, 18 Jun 2026](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/18/noam-shazeer-google-openai-characterai)

## 7. The debate
Publishing the Transformer let the whole field build on it, including Google's rivals. Keeping it secret might have kept Google ahead, or slowed AI down for everyone. Which would you have chosen?
