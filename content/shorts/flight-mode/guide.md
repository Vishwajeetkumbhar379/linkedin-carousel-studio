# Can your phone crash a plane? The 1-page guide to flight mode

Read this in 3 minutes and you will know what flight mode is really for, what the rules say and where they are changing.
Sources linked under every point, checked 24 Oct 2026 · @buildwithvish · October 2026

## 1. The short version
No documented crash has been blamed on a passenger's phone. The US ban on phones in the air is mainly about the mobile network on the ground, and the FAA says most airliners can tolerate the interference. The worst case pilots describe is an annoying buzz in their headsets.

## 2. Why your phone misbehaves up there
On the ground your phone talks to one nearby tower. In the air it can reach many towers at once and keeps switching between them as the plane moves, which can interfere with the ground network.
Source: [AFAR, 28 Aug 2026](https://www.afar.com/magazine/what-does-airplane-mode-do-and-why-do-you-need-to-use-it)

## 3. The 1991 rule
The US Federal Communications Commission adopted its ban on airborne mobile phone use in 1991 because of "harmful interference" to ground networks. The rule says phones must be turned off once an aircraft leaves the ground.
Sources: [US DOT, Federal Register, 24 Feb 2014](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/docs/Cell%20phone%20ANPRM%20-%20Fed%20Reg.pdf) · [47 CFR 22.925](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/22.925)

## 4. What the FAA says about the plane
In 2013 the FAA allowed phones in airplane mode in every phase of flight after experts concluded that "most commercial airplanes can tolerate radio interference signals" from personal devices. Voice calls stay banned under FCC rules.
Source: [FAA press release, 31 Oct 2013](https://apfa.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/103113FAA-PED-Release.pdf)

## 5. The real nuisance: the buzz
An airline pilot explained that phones searching for towers can put "a really annoying buzz" in cockpit headsets, "like a mosquito in my ear", while pilots copy instructions. A nuisance, not a catastrophe.
Source: [NBC Chicago, 11 Dec 2024](https://nbcchicago.com/news/local/not-a-conspiracy-pilot-shares-psa-on-the-real-reason-you-should-put-your-phone-on-airplane-mode-while-flying/3622275)

## 6. Europe already said yes
The European Commission's decision lets airlines offer 5G on board through a small onboard base station (a pico-cell), usually linked to the ground by satellite.
Source: [European Commission, 24 Nov 2022](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/5g-planes-wi-fi-road-commission-decision-opens-new-opportunities-innovation)

## 7. What to do on your next flight
- Follow the crew. The rules on your flight are the airline's and the country's, and they still apply.
- Switch on flight mode, then turn Wi-Fi and Bluetooth back on if the airline allows it.
- Bonus: your battery lasts longer because the phone stops searching for towers.
