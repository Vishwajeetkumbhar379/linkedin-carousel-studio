# Does the close door button do anything? The 1-page guide to placebo buttons

Read this in 3 minutes and you will know what the close door button really does, why, and where it actually works.
Sources linked under every point, checked 28 Oct 2026 · @buildwithvish · October 2026

## 1. The short version
In many US lifts the close door button can't shut the doors any faster than a legal minimum, and some were switched off for everyone except firefighters and engineers. In the UK, most close buttons do work. New York's crossing buttons were mostly placebos too.

## 2. What happens when you press it
The button sends a signal to the lift's controller. In many US lifts the controller won't close the doors until a minimum hold time has passed, so pressing it during that time does nothing you can see.
Source: [CBS Boston, citing The New York Times, 27 Oct 2016](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/door-close-elevator-button-dont-work-placebo)

## 3. The 1990 law behind it
After the Americans with Disabilities Act (1990), lift doors must stay open long enough for people using crutches, canes or wheelchairs to get in. The National Elevator Industry told the Times the close door function "faded into obsolescence" a few years later: "the riding public would not be able to make those doors close any faster."
Sources: [CBS Boston, 27 Oct 2016](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/door-close-elevator-button-dont-work-placebo) · [Drexel University on the NYT piece, 27 Oct 2016](https://drexel.edu/news/in-the-news/2016/October/Oct-27-Kounios-NYT)

## 4. The 3-second rule
The 1991 ADA Standards for Accessible Design say: "The minimum time for elevator doors to remain fully open in response to a car call shall be 3 seconds." Calls from the landing get a longer, distance-based time.
Source: [US Access Board, ADA Standards 1991, section 4.10.8](https://www.access-board.gov/adaag-1991-2002.html)

## 5. Who the button still works for
Firefighters and maintenance staff with the right key can use it. In fire service mode the doors don't close by themselves, so the close button is essential, and it is tested at inspections. That is why some engineers say the "it does nothing" claim is overstated: after the minimum hold, many US buttons do close the doors.
Sources: [CBS Boston, 27 Oct 2016](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/door-close-elevator-button-dont-work-placebo) · [computer.rip, 13 Mar 2023](https://computer.rip/2023-03-13-the-door-close-button.html)

## 6. It's not just lifts: New York's crossing buttons
In 2004 The New York Times found that "more than 2,500 of the 3,250 walk buttons that still exist function essentially as mechanical placebos", left over after the city moved to computer-timed signals.
Source: [Reason, quoting The New York Times, 27 Feb 2004](https://reason.com/2004/02/27/buttons-down/)

## 7. In the UK and Europe
The Telegraph reported that close door buttons are fully functional in most UK lifts. A Lift and Escalator Industry Association spokesman said not every lift has one, but where they exist they work; how long the doors wait still varies by lift.
Source: [Mental Floss, citing The Telegraph, 22 Jun 2018](https://mentalfloss.com/article/547416/do-close-door-buttons-elevators-actually-do-anything)
