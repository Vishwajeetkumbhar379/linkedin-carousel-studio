# Does Incognito mode hide you? The 1-page guide

Read this in 3 minutes and you will know what Incognito really does, who can still see you and what Google agreed to in court.
Sources linked under every point, checked 28 Oct 2026 · @buildwithvish · October 2026

## 1. The short version
Incognito only stops your own browser from keeping a record once you close the window. Your network, your employer or school, your internet provider and the websites you visit can still see what you do. In 2024 Google agreed to delete or anonymise billions of records to settle a lawsuit about it.

## 2. What Incognito actually does
Chrome keeps cookies and site data only while the Incognito window is open. When you close it, Chrome doesn't retain site data or a record of the sites you visited. Bookmarks you save and files you download stay.
Source: [Google Chrome Help, "How Chrome Incognito keeps your browsing private"](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95464?hl=en-GB)

## 3. Who can still see you
Google's own help page says websites you visit, including Google sites, and organisations that manage your network, like your school, employer or internet service provider, may be able to observe your activity in Incognito.
Source: [Google Chrome Help](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95464?hl=en-GB)

## 4. The $5 billion lawsuit
In 2020, users sued Google (Brown v. Google, US District Court, Northern District of California) for $5 billion. They said Google's analytics, cookies and apps kept tracking people who used private browsing modes.
Source: [NBC News, 1 Apr 2024](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/google-destroy-browsing-data-settle-consumer-privacy-lawsuit-rcna145881)

## 5. What Google's own staff said
Court documents seen by Bloomberg showed an engineer writing in a 2018 chat: "We need to stop calling it Incognito and stop using a Spy Guy icon." Marketing chief Lorraine Twohill wrote to CEO Sundar Pichai: "Make Incognito Mode truly private."
Source: [Gizmodo, 12 Oct 2022](https://gizmodo.com/google-incognito-mode-google-chrome-1849648071)

## 6. The 2024 settlement
Terms filed on 1 April 2024: Google agreed to destroy or de-identify billions of data records, rewrite its Incognito disclosures and let Incognito users block third-party cookies for five years. The class of about 136 million US users gets no damages, but people can still sue individually. Google said the plaintiffs "originally wanted $5 billion and are receiving zero". The opening screen changed from "now you can browse privately" to "you can browse more privately".
Source: [GovTech / Bloomberg, 3 Apr 2024](https://www.govtech.com/security/google-to-purge-stored-incognito-mode-records)

## 7. What to do instead
- Use Incognito for what it is good at: keeping a shared device free of your history.
- On a work or school network or device, assume your employer or school can see your traffic.
- Signing in to a site inside Incognito tells that site exactly who you are.
- For privacy from your network or provider, look at tools built for that (a trusted VPN or Tor), and read what each one actually promises.
