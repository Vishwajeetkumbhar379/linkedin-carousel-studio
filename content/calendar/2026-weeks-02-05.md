# 4-week calendar: Weeks 2 to 5 of 52 (27 Oct to 22 Nov 2026)

The system (`PLAYBOOK.md` §2): 3 feed posts a week, one theme. Mon is Carousel A (how-to), Thu is the Reel (the "Week N of 52" episode), Sat is Carousel B (brand-side insight). Times start at 18:30 Berlin.

**Filming:** one session every 2 weeks.
- **Weekend 25 to 26 Oct:** Week 2 and Week 3 episodes.
- **Weekend 8 to 9 Nov:** Week 4 and Week 5 episodes. Their scripts get written after Week 3's real results, because an episode only promises what really happened.

Status key:
- **READY:** files rendered.
- **SCRIPT:** shoot sheet ready, footage needed.
- **PLAN:** topic and resource set, to be built.
- **LIVE:** the guide or DM link already works.
- **BRANCH:** works once the buildwithvish branch is merged.

## Rules this calendar keeps
- Every hook is 12 words or fewer. Real numbers only; brackets mean "Vish gives the number".
- Each week's carousels teach what the episode lives. No point is repeated twice in one week.
- Every keyword has a resource before its post goes up. New keywords (PITCH, INSTA) get a guide and a `config.json` entry first.
- **Every 2 weeks (Mon 10 Nov, Mon 24 Nov), check the buckets:**
  - the documentary;
  - AI workflows;
  - brand-side.

  Triple down on the winner (Kallaway's 3-2-1).
- Never his past relationship; never a promise the next episode can't keep.
