## 1. The rule that makes the volume possible
**One filming session, many posts.** Every session gives at least:
- 1 episode;
- 2 shorts cut from his walk-and-talks and asides;
- 1 or 2 cutdowns of any 3D explainer;
- 2 carousels built from the episode's real work.

**Clone voice for the gaps (Vish, 27 Oct).** Lines he didn't film are spoken by his own ElevenLabs clone ("Young indian male"). The clone gets at most 2 lines per reel; his own takes always carry the hook. Clone-voiced reels get Instagram's "AI info" label.

**B-roll matches the line.** "If you're saying beach, then the beach should be there." In order of preference:
1. His own phone clips.
2. Real archive footage from movingimagearchive.com: public domain, free to reuse, search by sentence. Checked 27 Oct.
3. An animated graphic.

Never an unrelated clip.
