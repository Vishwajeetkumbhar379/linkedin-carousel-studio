MON 2 NOV · IMAGE: trend-posts/agent-skills-280m.png (or .mp4)

280 million installs. Of a text file.

On 22 October, Anthropic donated Agent Skills to the Agentic AI Foundation, under the Linux Foundation. A skill is a folder with one SKILL.md file that teaches an AI agent one specific job, plus any scripts or reference it needs.

Why marketers should care: 1 million skills have been published and nearly 280 million installed. More than 70 agent platforms read them, including ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and VS Code. Write a skill once and it works almost everywhere.

I'm a marketer, not an engineer, and I use skills every day. Mine cover job scouting, applications, carousels, creator shortlists and contract reviews.

The best ones are boring: one task, written down properly, done the same way every time.

If you write one this month, write it for the task you explain to people most often.

Comment SKILLS and I'll send you my list.

THU 5 NOV · IMAGE: trend-posts/instagram-90-min.png (or .mp4)

Instagram is testing 90-minute videos. From the brand side, this changes more than the format.

The facts first. On 26 October, Instagram started testing Features: horizontal, long-form videos of up to 90 minutes, with a small group of creators in the US.

I've worked on 850+ creator deals on the brand side. Here's what I'd expect if it rolls out:

1. Integrations come back. A reel has room for a mention. A 30-minute video has room for a segment.
2. Usage rights get messier. Brands will want to cut that segment into ads. Creators should price that separately.
3. Rate cards need a new line. Long-form isn't a reel times ten. Price it on the work and the audience, not the minutes.

Creators: don't change your format for a test. Do add a long-form line to your rate card before a brand asks.

Brands: would you pay more for a long-form integration than for a reel?

#InfluencerMarketing #CreatorEconomy #Instagram #BrandDeals
