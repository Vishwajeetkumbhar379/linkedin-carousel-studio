1. MON | ONE MEETING COST ME SIX MONTHS (story)
On screen hook: "One meeting. Six months."

For four years, I worked remotely from India.
Then one meeting cancelled my next two flights.
And I was stuck in a nine to five. For six months.
For someone obsessed with travel? That was hell.
Here's what it taught me.
Remote isn't freedom when you're paid in a currency that doesn't stretch.
And it isn't freedom when one meeting can call you back.
So I'm fixing both. Income in euros. A job that can't call me back.
In December, I work from Sri Lanka for a month.
Follow to see if I actually make it.

On screen hook: "Bali has a coach problem."

Hot take. In Bali, every next person is a coach.
I don't have a problem with coaches.

I don't want to be just another creator with a ring light and a course.
I want a stable income, and the freedom to experiment.
Different skills. Different countries. Different genres.
Bali is beautiful. The people are lovely.
But it gets repetitive fast, if you're not smart.
So here's my rule. Build the skill first. The location second.
