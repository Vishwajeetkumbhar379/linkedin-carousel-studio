WHY THIS SHAPE (from the research, 28 Oct)
- Paritosh's best videos use a statement, a number and a time box ("give me 19 minutes"). His weakest titles are questions. So the episode opens with a statement and a real number.
- Kallaway: name the topic in five words, then turn it the other way. Put a free thing behind one keyword. "The script format" got 20,172 comments that way.
- Every big account we studied shows the proof on screen, so you show the tests, not just talk about them.

THE THREE TESTS (each one is a scene in the episode)

1. Setup test (Thu 29 to Fri 30 Oct)
- Time yourself from "I want to record" to pressing record, with a stopwatch on screen. Do it once before you change anything.
- Build the corner, then time it again. The two real numbers go in the episode.
- The tripod and mic are your call and your money. The free version: phone on a stack of books by the window, lamp on, same seat every time.
- B-roll (5 s each): the corner before, building it (three quick shots), the corner after, you sitting down and hitting record.

2. Job search test (all week)
- Let the automation run every morning.
- Each evening, read the drafts and send only the ones you'd sign.
- Keep a tally in your notes app: roles found, drafts made, applications sent, replies.
- B-roll: one morning's shortlist on screen (scroll it; I blur the company names), you changing a line in a cover letter, you hitting send.

3. Portfolio test (from Mon 2 Nov)
- Put buildwithvvish.com/#portfolio in your Instagram bio, your LinkedIn featured section and every application.
- Note your LinkedIn profile views and Instagram link taps on Monday, then again on Wednesday.
- B-roll: the phone opening your portfolio.
