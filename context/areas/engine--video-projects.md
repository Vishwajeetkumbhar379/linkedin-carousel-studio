# engine/video-projects

Produce, edit, and assemble week‑01 video project files via Python, JSON, and HTML.

- `video-projects/edit-test/broll.json`: JSON: _note, clips, pages, music
- `video-projects/week-01-intro/edit/comp.html`: week 1 comp
- `video-projects/week-01-intro/edit/week1.py`: Week 1 intro script: prep cuts, stills, render, mix; uses transcript, audio beats, ffmpeg, Playwright. (uses grade.py, film.py, comp.html)
