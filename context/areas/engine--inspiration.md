# engine/inspiration

Collect, document, and analyze external design inspirations to inform style rules and content generation.

- `inspiration/log.md`: Logs source URLs, licenses, learned patterns, and usage decisions for inspiration repositories. (uses night.html)
- `inspiration/moodboard/README.md`: Describes moodboard workflow, reference sources, and screenshot naming for design inspiration.
- `inspiration/style-directions.md`: Defines five carousel style directions, locked rules, sample references, and risk notes. (uses tokens.json)
- `inspiration/teardowns/instagram-references.md`: Lists Instagram post metrics, captions, CTA patterns, and extraction notes for future use.
- `inspiration/teardowns/vish-references-2026-10-06.md`: Analyzes Vish screenshots, extracts premium patterns, and adds new design rules.
