- `content/shorts/gpt-t/script.json`: JSON: slug, series, episode, template, renderer, voice, hook_type, lines
- `content/shorts/hit-send/guide.md`: Explains ChatGPT request flow, tokens, cost, and usage tips.
- `content/shorts/hit-send/script.json`: JSON: slug, series, episode, template, voice, lines, facts, publish
