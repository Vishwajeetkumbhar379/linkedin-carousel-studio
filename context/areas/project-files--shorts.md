# project-files/shorts

Organize research, transcripts, metadata, and guides to produce data‑driven YouTube Shorts efficiently.

- `shorts/guide-PAPER.md`: One-page summary of the Transformer paper, authors, patent, career moves, and $2.7B deal.
- `shorts/guide-TOKENS.md`: Explains tokens, AI chip price, energy use, politeness cost, and prompt optimization in three minutes.
- `shorts/research/SUMMARY.md`: Provides measured and third-party stats, methodology, and limits for analyzing top YouTube Shorts. (uses top-reels.json)
- `shorts/research/reference-De0BAl8B3mY/TEARDOWN.md`: Documents full teardown of 'How we taught sand to think' reel: files, stats, voice, hook, pacing. (uses transcript.json)
- `shorts/research/reference-De0BAl8B3mY/meta.json`: JSON: id, title, description, uploader, uploader_id, channel, like_count, view_count
- `shorts/research/reference-De0BAl8B3mY/sound-notes-gemini.md`: Breaks down narrator voice, music, and detailed SFX timeline for the 141-second reel narration.
- `shorts/research/reference-De0BAl8B3mY/transcript.json`: JSON: language, segments
- `shorts/research/top-reels.json`: JSON: generated, method, provenance, previous_web_fallback, accounts (uses ig_fetch.py)
- `shorts/voice-samples/samples.json`: JSON: list of 8
