LinkedIn just killed its own AI writer.

The old "enhance your post" button that rewrote your draft is gone. In its place: Post Proofreader, for eligible Premium members.

What it does:
→ fixes spelling, grammar and punctuation
→ shortens a post while keeping the meaning
→ makes it clearer
→ shows every change tracked, so you approve it first

Why now? LinkedIn's new "report AI slop" option was used more than one million times in under three weeks.

That tells me two things. Members are tired of posts that sound like nobody. And LinkedIn would rather polish your words than invent new ones.

My honest take: I use AI every day in my marketing work. But the first draft has to be mine, even if it's messy. AI is great at tidying. It's poor at having an opinion you actually hold.

So: draft ugly, in your own voice. Then let a tool fix the commas.
