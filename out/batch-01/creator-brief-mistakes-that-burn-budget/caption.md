Five brief mistakes that quietly burn creator budget.

850+ creator deals in, the expensive problems rarely came from the creator. They came from the brief.

The five I'd fix first:
→ Writing the script for them. You hired a voice, then muted it.
→ Six key messages in a 30-second video. One lands. Maybe two.
→ No approved claims list. Legal finds out at draft stage, when changes cost most.
→ No single approver. Four people, four directions, round three.
→ Hiding that it'll run as a paid ad. Creators film differently for paid use, and you pay twice for edits.

None of these look expensive on the day you send the brief. They show up as a reshoot, a late post, or content that sounds like an ad.

I've made some of these myself. The fix is usually one line in the brief.
