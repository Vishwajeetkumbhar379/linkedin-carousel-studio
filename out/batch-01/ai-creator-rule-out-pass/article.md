# Use AI to rule creators out, then write the first line

Most people ask AI to *find* creators. That's the part it's worst at. It can't see private audience data, it doesn't know who's open to brand deals, and its suggestions often lean towards names it has read about rather than the right fit for your brief.

After 850+ creator deals, I've found the expensive mistakes rarely come from missing a great creator. They come from booking a wrong one. So I use AI for the opposite job: ruling creators out, fast, with evidence. Then I use it to draft the first line of outreach for the ones who survive.

## Pass 1: Hard filters (you, not AI)

These are yes or no, and you already know them:

- Audience country and language match the market
- Rate range fits the budget
- Platform and format match the deliverables

Do this in a sheet. It's quick and doesn't need a model.

## Pass 2: The rule-out prompt

For each creator still standing, copy their recent public captions (around a dozen) and two or three comment threads into the chat. Paste only public content and nothing private about the creator or their followers.

```
Here's my campaign brief: [paste brief]
Here are [creator]'s last 12 captions and 3 comment threads: [paste]

Your job is to find reasons NOT to work with this creator for this brief.
Check:
1. Tone: would our product feel out of place next to these posts?
2. Category: any recent paid posts for direct competitors?
3. Audience: do the comments suggest a different audience than the brief targets?
4. Comment quality: generic, repeated or off-topic comments.
5. Brand safety: anything we'd need to discuss before signing.

For each point: verdict (fine / check / problem) and a quote as evidence.
If you can't tell from what I gave you, say "can't tell", don't guess.
```

The "quote as evidence" line is the important one. It turns vague AI opinions into something you can check in ten seconds.

## Pass 3: Check the flags yourself

AI is fast at spotting a tone clash or a competitor mention. It is not reliable at judging whether comments are bought, and it can misread sarcasm or in-jokes. Treat every "problem" as a question, not a verdict. Open the post. Read the thread. Ask the creator for audience screenshots if fit is unclear.

If you want a scored version of this, my [creator shortlist rubric](https://buildwithvish.netlify.app/#read-shortlist-creators-with-ai) weights fit, engagement health, cost and safety.

## Pass 4: The first line

The creators who make it through deserve outreach that shows you actually watched their content. This is where AI helps again, as long as you rewrite the result.

```
Here's [creator]'s post about [topic]: [paste caption or describe it].
Write 3 options for the first line of a DM that:
- names this specific post and one concrete thing that worked in it
- is under 20 words
- has no flattery words (amazing, love your content, obsessed)
```

Pick one, then rewrite it in your own words. Add the offer in one sentence and an easy next step. The full three-line structure is in [Creator outreach that actually gets replies](https://buildwithvish.netlify.app/#read-creator-outreach-that-gets-replies).

## What I'd do

- Keep Pass 1 in a shared sheet so the team agrees on hard filters before anyone opens a chat.
- Save the rule-out prompt in a Claude or ChatGPT Project with your brief already loaded, so each creator is one paste.
- Log every "problem" you confirmed by hand. After a few campaigns you'll know which flags the AI gets right for your niche, and which ones it overcalls.
- Never send the AI's first line unedited. Creators can tell.
