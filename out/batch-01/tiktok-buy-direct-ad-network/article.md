# TikTok wants shoppers to buy from the For You feed in one click

## TikTok's plan: close the sale inside the feed

At Advertising Week New York 2026, TikTok unveiled a package of AI driven advertising and commerce products. Taken together, they point in one direction: discovery, questions and checkout, without leaving TikTok.

## What TikTok announced

**Buy Direct.** An in-app checkout. According to TikTok, users can buy from a brand without leaving the app, from the For You feed with a single click.

**Shopping Assistant.** A conversational AI agent that, TikTok says, remembers what a user needs and gives real-time guidance on product details, shipping, sizing, availability and purchasing in one conversation.

**Agentic Leads.** An AI agent for lead generation that "will be available" as a Lead Agent in TikTok Direct Messages and in lead forms on advertisers' own websites.

**TikTok Ad Network for US advertisers.** Campaigns can extend beyond TikTok to a network of **nearly 400,000 apps** worldwide, managed inside TikTok Ads Manager.

**TikTok for Business MCP as a connector.** TikTok's ads interface is now available inside AI platforms including Claude, Perplexity, Replit and Manus. TikTok says the number of advertisers using the MCP has risen by **over 200%** since launch, based on internal data. It didn't give a starting number, so treat that figure carefully.

## The catch

PPC Land pointed out that the announcement contains **no pricing and no dated rollout schedule** for any of the new items. Buy Direct, for example, has no stated start date, no list of markets and no detail on what buyer data reaches the brand.

So this is a direction of travel, not a switch you can flip tomorrow.

## Why it matters for marketers

For years the social commerce funnel looked like this: video, profile, link, website, cart, checkout. Every step lost people. TikTok is trying to collapse that into: video, one click.

If it works, two things change:

1. **Your product page data matters more than your landing page design.** An AI assistant answering "will this fit?" needs clear sizing, shipping and stock information to work with.
2. **Creative becomes the checkout.** The video isn't the top of the funnel any more. It might be the whole funnel.

## What I'd do

- **Audit your product data.** Sizes, materials, delivery times, returns. Write them clearly, in one place.
- **Prepare a FAQ for the assistant era.** List the ten questions customers ask your support team before buying. Make sure every answer is public.
- **Test the ad network carefully.** If you're a US advertiser, start with a small budget and compare results to in-feed placements.
- **Try the MCP connector for reporting first.** Asking an assistant to summarise campaign performance is a low-risk way to learn the tool.
- **Don't bet the quarter on undated features.** Plan for them. Budget for them when they have a date.

Having worked on 850+ creator deals, I've seen how often a great video lost the sale at the link in bio. One-click checkout from the feed goes straight at that problem. I'll believe the impact when brands report results.
