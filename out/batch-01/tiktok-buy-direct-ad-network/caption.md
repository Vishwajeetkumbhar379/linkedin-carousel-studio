TikTok wants shoppers to buy straight from the For You feed.

At Advertising Week New York, TikTok showed its AI ad and commerce plans:
→ Buy Direct: buy from a brand in one click, without leaving the app
→ Shopping Assistant: an AI agent for sizing, shipping and stock questions
→ Lead agents in DMs and on advertiser lead forms
→ TikTok Ad Network for US advertisers: nearly 400,000 outside apps
→ TikTok's ads tools as a connector inside assistants like Claude and Perplexity

The direction is clear. Discovery, questions and checkout, all in one place.

The catch: most of these tools have no launch date and no pricing yet.

What I'd do: don't rebuild your funnel this week. But check your product pages answer the questions a shopping assistant will be asked: size, delivery time, stock, returns. If the AI can't find the answer, it can't sell for you.
