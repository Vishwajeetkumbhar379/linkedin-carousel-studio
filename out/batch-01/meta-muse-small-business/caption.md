Meta's AI agent now plugs into your shop, books and ads.

It's called Muse for Small Business. What it connects to:
→ Shopify, Stripe, Canva, QuickBooks
→ Instagram business and Facebook Page analytics
→ Meta ad accounts
→ Slack, Zoom, Notion, Figma, Dropbox

Owners can ask it to look at sales and campaigns, plan growth, draft customer messages and create ads.

The rule I like: it won't post, send or buy anything without the owner's approval.

And a telling number: Meta says roughly one in three early Muse users had already connected it to a business account.

My honest take: the agent is free for most uses. Your brand voice isn't. If you hand it a vague brief, you'll get vague ads, faster. The businesses that win here are the ones that write down who they sell to and how they sound, before the agent starts.
