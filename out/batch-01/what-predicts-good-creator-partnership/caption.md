The best creator partners ask questions before they quote.

850+ creator deals in, I've stopped trusting the media kit as the first signal. The first reply tells me more.

The partnerships that went well usually started with a creator who:
→ asked who the campaign was for and what it needed to achieve
→ asked what they weren't allowed to say
→ pushed back on part of the brief, because they knew their audience better than we did
→ told us honestly what hadn't worked for them before
→ had turned down brands that didn't fit, and said so

The ones that went badly often started with a rate card and nothing else.

None of this shows up in follower count. It shows up in one email.

This is my experience, not a study. I'd love to hear the other side.
