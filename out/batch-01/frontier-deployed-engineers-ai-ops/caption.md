Anthropic is putting $100M into one job title.

On 2 October, Anthropic launched Claude Frontier Academy. The goal: train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by the end of 2027. First cohorts include engineers from Accenture, Deloitte, McKinsey, Commonwealth Bank and others.

What the training looks like:
→ A simulated enterprise rollout: choose the use case, get through security review, hand it over
→ A graded practical
→ 12 weeks leading a real Claude project inside their own organisation

To be clear: it's for software engineers, by nomination. I'm a marketer moving into AI Ops, not an engineer, so this programme isn't for me.

But the shape of the job is the interesting part. Pick the right problem. Get it through review safely. Make sure people actually use it. Two of those three are things good marketers and project leads do every week.

The code is the part I'm still learning. The rest I've been doing on creator campaigns for years.
