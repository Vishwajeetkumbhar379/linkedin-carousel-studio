AI creators are cheap. Trust still isn't.

AI avatar ads keep getting better. No contracts, no shipping product, no waiting for drafts, no reshoots. After 850+ creator deals, I understand the appeal more than most. Chasing approvals is real work.

But I think brands are mixing up two different things.

A creator isn't paid for a face reading a script. They're paid for an audience that believes them, built over years of posts, comments and the brands they turned down.

An avatar has none of that:
→ no comment section history
→ no record of saying no
→ no one who'd notice if it changed its mind

Where I'd use AI avatars: testing hooks and angles cheaply before spending on creators, explainer content, internal training.

Where I'd still pay a person: anything where the viewer has to believe the recommendation.

And if you use an avatar, label it clearly. Hiding it borrows trust you didn't earn.

Where do you draw the line?
