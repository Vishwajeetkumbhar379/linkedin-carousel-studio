You can now brief Google's ad AI in German.

Google is expanding the closed beta of AI Brief, the tool that lets you guide AI Max in your own words, to 7 more languages:
→ Dutch, French, German, Italian
→ Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish

AI Brief takes context on your business, your audience and your key messages, and uses it to steer AI Max for Search.

Google also announced a new report: one view of the Search ads journey, from the search term, to the creative the person saw, to the page they landed on. No general availability date yet.

As someone working in AI and marketing from Germany, this is the part I like: you can now write the brief in the language your customers actually search in.

My take: the brief is becoming the most important asset in paid search. A vague brief gives the AI vague instructions, just in more languages.
