# Google's AI Max now takes briefs in German, French and five more languages

## Google's AI Max now speaks seven more languages

On 23 September, Google announced two updates for **AI Max for Search**, its AI driven Search campaign feature.

1. The closed beta of **AI Brief** is expanding to **Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Spanish**.
2. A new **unified reporting** view for the Search ads journey.

For European advertisers, the first one matters a lot.

## What AI Brief does

In Google's words, AI Brief "lets you guide AI Max in your own words with detailed context on your business, audience, and key messaging."

In practice, it's a written brief for the machine. Instead of only feeding the system keywords, assets and a URL, you tell it who you serve, what you offer and what you want to say. Until now, that was limited in language. Now advertisers can write it in seven more languages, including German and French.

It's still a **closed beta**, so not every account will see it.

## The new report

Google is also introducing a reporting feature that shows a single view of the Search ads journey:

- which **search terms** triggered your ads
- which **creative assets** the user saw
- where they **landed** on your website

That's useful because AI Max decides a lot on its own, from matching to creative. Seeing the full path in one place makes it easier to check whether the AI's choices make sense for your business. Google hasn't announced when the report will be generally available.

## Why the brief is becoming the main asset

When AI picks the queries and assembles the ads, your control shifts upstream. The brief is where your strategy lives now. A good brief is specific about audience, offer and boundaries. A weak one gives the AI room to guess.

That's also why language matters. A German brief can use the actual words German customers search with, instead of a translation of an English idea.

## What I'd do

- **Write the brief in your customers' language,** not your head office's language.
- **Include what you don't want.** Products you don't sell, claims you can't make, audiences that aren't a fit.
- **Name your top three messages.** In priority order, in plain words.
- **Add proof points.** Delivery times, guarantees, certifications. Real ones only.
- **Review the new report monthly** once it's available: search term, creative, landing page. If any link in that chain looks wrong, fix the brief first.

My limit: I don't have access data for this beta, so I can't tell you how much a brief changes results. Treat it as a test with a control period.
