AI won't replace your content team. A bad brief will.

Here's the pattern I worry about.

A team gets a vague brief. "Make it engaging. Drive awareness. Something for Gen Z." Average work comes back, because average is what a vague brief produces.

Then the same brief goes into an AI tool. Average work comes back again, just faster and cheaper.

Someone looks at the pile and decides the team was the problem.

It wasn't. Nobody decided:
→ the one goal
→ the one audience
→ the one thing to say
→ what success looks like

In my experience, teams that brief well get more out of AI, not less work. The brief is the skill that makes both people and tools good.

I learnt this writing creator briefs long before I wrote prompts. Same failure, same fix.
