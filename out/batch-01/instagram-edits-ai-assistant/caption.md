Instagram's new AI reads your Reels data, then pitches ideas.

It's called the Edits assistant, inside Instagram's Edits app. It looks at:
→ follows, views and retention
→ likes and shares
→ your comments
→ what's trending and what your audience is into

Then it suggests what to make next, based on your own track record rather than a generic trend list.

Instagram's line on it: the assistant does the digging, the creative calls stay with the creator.

The catch: free use is capped. More usage sits behind a Meta One subscription.
