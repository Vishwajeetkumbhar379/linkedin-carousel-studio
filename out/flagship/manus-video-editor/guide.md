# AI UGC ad test with Manus: the prompts I'd start with (for everyone who commented MANUS)

What Manus says it can do (Manus blog, 1 Oct 2026): make a whole video from one or two sentences, including AI UGC-style ads, and put every piece (shots, captions, voiceover, music, sound effects) on its own track in Video Editor, inside Manus Studio, the desktop app. Check Manus's pricing page for what your plan includes.

## The test, in three steps
1. Make three versions of the same 20-second ad, one per angle.
2. Post them (or run them as small paid tests) and compare saves, comments and clicks.
3. Brief a real creator on the angle that won. AI tests the angle; a real creator earns the trust.

## Prompt 1: three angles
"Make three vertical 20-second AI UGC-style ads for [product], filmed as if by a real customer on a phone. Angle A: the problem it solves. Angle B: unboxing and first use. Angle C: before and after. Same product, same length, same music style, captions on. Keep every piece on its own track."

## Prompt 2: your brand, not a generic one
"Use our brand: [colours, tone of voice, three words that describe us]. The first line on screen must stop the scroll in two seconds. End with [call to action]."

## Prompt 3: the fix-it pass (in Video Editor)
"Tighten the first three seconds, put the product in frame by second two, lower the music under the voice, and rewrite the caption on the last shot to [new line]. Don't change anything else."

## Prompt 4: the creator brief (after the test)
"Turn the winning ad into a brief for a real creator: the hook line, the three moments that must appear, what not to say, deliverables, and usage rights we need."

## What I'd watch out for
- Label AI-generated ads as AI where the platform or law requires it.
- Never present an AI person as a real customer review.
- Use AI to choose the angle; pay a real creator for the version you scale.
