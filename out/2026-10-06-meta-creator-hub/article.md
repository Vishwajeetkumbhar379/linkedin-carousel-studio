# Meta's Creator Marketing Hub: one-click creator ads, and what still needs a human

In mid-September 2026 Meta launched the Creator Marketing Hub. It brings Creator Marketplace and the Partnership Ads Hub into one place, so a brand can find creators, agree permissions and turn a creator's post into an ad from the same screen.

## What's inside

- Creator discovery and content search in one hub
- Content-level permissions with expiry dates
- One-click ad creation from creator posts
- Messaging creators inside the hub
- Facebook creators added to the Creator Marketplace API, which until now only covered Instagram creators

## What a faster button doesn't fix

Speed was never the bottleneck in creator campaigns. The slow, risky parts are the decisions around the post:

- **Usage rights.** How long, which channels, which markets. Write it down before anything is boosted.
- **Fit.** Would your product look natural in the creator's last 12 posts? If not, no ad format will save it.
- **Disclosure.** In Germany, paid content is usually labelled "Werbung" or "Anzeige" at the start of the caption. This is general information, not legal advice.
- **Payment.** When the creator gets paid, and what triggers it.

## What I'd do

1. Add an expiry date and a channel list to every creator brief, so the new permission settings match what you agreed.
2. Shortlist on fit first, reach second.
3. Use one-click ads for testing which creator post works as an ad, then scale the winner.

Faster tools make the brief matter more.
