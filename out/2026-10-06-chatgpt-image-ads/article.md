# ChatGPT is testing ads next to generated images: what marketers should know

On 5 October 2026 OpenAI announced a new visual ad format that appears while people generate images in ChatGPT. Testing starts later this month in the US with a first group of advertisers.

## How it works

- Ads are labelled and sit beside the generated image, not inside it.
- OpenAI says ads don't influence ChatGPT's answers.
- Measurement partners are already connected, including AppsFlyer, Northbeam and Triple Whale.

## Why it matters

OpenAI says 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT every week. Image generation is a moment of intent: someone is picturing a product, a room, a look or a campaign. That's a new kind of ad slot.

## The early number, read carefully

OpenAI shared one partner-reported result: WeightWatchers' attributed cost per acquisition on ChatGPT Ads was 15.3% lower than its blended paid-search benchmark, measured by DV Rockerbox. One brand, one partner, attributed numbers. Treat it as a signal, not proof.

## What I'd do

1. If you're US-based and spend on search, ask your agency or OpenAI rep about the test now.
2. Prepare image-first creative: the ad sits next to a picture someone just asked for.
3. Decide what proof you'd need (incrementality, not just attributed CPA) before moving real budget.
