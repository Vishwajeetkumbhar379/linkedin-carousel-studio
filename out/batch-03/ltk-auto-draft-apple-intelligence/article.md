# Can Apple Intelligence Write Your Captions? A Walkthrough of LTK Auto-Draft

## Overview
LTK, known for powering over $6 billion in annual retail sales and supporting more than 60,000 creator-brand collaborations each year, has introduced Auto-Draft. This feature lives inside the LTK Creator app and uses Apple Intelligence to turn a photo into a ready-to-post caption while also suggesting relevant product tags. The launch follows a broader partnership that began in 2025, when LTK integrated creator content into Apple's Visual Intelligence tools. Auto-Draft is available to creators who have an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence running iOS 27. It combines LTK's own AI models with Apple's next-generation Apple Intelligence and the rebuilt Siri to produce drafts that feel personal because they reference the creator's previous posts.

## How Auto-Draft Works
The process is straightforward and can be started from three places: the LTK Creator app, the Photos app, or a Siri shortcut.
1. Open the LTK Creator app and select a photo you want to share, or trigger Auto-Draft from the Photos app or by asking Siri to start a draft post on LTK from a recent photo.
2. Tap the Auto-Draft button. Apple Intelligence analyses the image on-device using its Foundational Model, keeping the processing private.
3. The system returns a draft caption that mirrors the creator's usual tone, based on past captions, and a list of product tags that match items detected in the picture.
4. Creators can choose from several tone options (e.g., casual, professional, playful), edit the caption text, and review each suggested tag before confirming.
5. Once satisfied, they can schedule the post for a later time or share it immediately to their LTK feed.

Because the AI runs locally, there is no need to upload the photo to external servers, which helps with privacy and speed.

## What I'd Do
I ran a quick test with five sample photos ranging from flat-lay fashion shots to lifestyle images. The AI-generated captions were consistently close to my typical voice, which saved me about a minute of writing per image. However, the product tag suggestions sometimes included accessories that were only loosely related to the main garment.

My personal workflow would look like this:
- Capture the photo and save it to the device.
- Open the Photos app, tap the share sheet, and choose the LTK Auto-Draft shortcut for a one-tap start.
- When the draft appears, I first read the caption and adjust any phrasing to better fit the campaign goal.
- I then go through the tag list, removing any items that are not clearly visible in the photo or that belong to a different brand.
- After confirming the tags, I either schedule the post for the evening peak time or share it right away if the content is timely.

This approach lets me enjoy the speed of AI while maintaining control over brand relevance and avoiding mismatched tags.
