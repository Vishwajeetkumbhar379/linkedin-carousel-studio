Volunteer influencer programme for Nevada governor’s race

Nevada Attorney General Aaron Ford’s gubernatorial campaign built a volunteer influencer programme called “Creators for Aaron”. The programme enlists content creators with follower counts ranging from the low thousands to the millions, and none of the participating creators are paid. Fifteen governor’s race videos reviewed had a combined 30,000 likes on Instagram and TikTok, and the six accounts behind those videos have more than 24 million followers between the platforms. Both the Ford and Lombardo campaigns launched TikTok accounts in March.
