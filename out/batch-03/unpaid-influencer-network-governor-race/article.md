# How Nevada’s governor campaign built a volunteer influencer network

## Introduction

Nevada Attorney General Aaron Ford’s gubernatorial campaign built a volunteer influencer programme called “Creators for Aaron”. The programme enlists content creators with follower counts ranging from the low thousands to the millions, and none of the participating creators are paid.

## What the source reports

Fifteen Nevada governor’s race videos reviewed had a combined 30,000 likes across Instagram and TikTok. The six accounts behind those videos carry more than 24 million followers between Instagram and TikTok. Both the Ford and Lombardo campaigns launched TikTok accounts in March.
