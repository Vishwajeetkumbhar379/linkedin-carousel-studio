# The best ChatGPT apps and connectors for marketers: step-by-step guide

## The best ChatGPT apps and connectors for marketers

Marketers spend too much time jumping between ChatGPT and their tools. The ChatGPT Apps marketplace and verified MCP servers let you bring data from ad platforms, CRMs and automation services straight into the chat. This removes the need to copy-paste or switch tabs while you draft campaigns, analyse performance or brainstorm ideas.

## Why connect apps inside ChatGPT

When you install a verified app or plugin, ChatGPT gains live access to external data. You can ask for real-time keyword volumes, pull CRM records or trigger a Zapier workflow, all from a single conversation. The setup is quick: you need a ChatGPT Plus plan to open up Developer Mode, then you add the app from the Settings → Apps menu.

## Step-by-step: how to switch on the top apps

### Ensure you have ChatGPT Plus

Developer Mode, required for MCP servers, is only available on Plus, Pro, Team, Enterprise and Edu plans. Free plans cannot use MCP servers. The Plus plan starts at $20 per month.

### Open Settings → Apps

In ChatGPT click your name, choose Settings, then select Apps. This opens the marketplace where you can search for verified connectors.

### Add Adspirer for multi-platform ad management

Search for Adspirer, click Connect and authorise your Google, Meta, Amazon, ChatGPT Ads, LinkedIn and TikTok accounts. Adspirer has been an official listing since May 29 2026 and connects to over 400 tools across six platforms. Since June 2026 it returns interactive in-chat dashboards with tabbed Overview, Campaigns and Insights views.

### Add HubSpot plugin for CRM

The HubSpot plugin lets you read contact lists, deal stages and trigger workflows using natural language. It works across claude.ai, Claude Desktop and other ChatGPT surfaces, giving you live CRM data without leaving the chat.

### Add Zapier plugin for workflow automation

With the Zapier plugin you describe an automation, for example “When a new lead arrives in HubSpot, add a row to Google Sheets”, and Zapier builds it inside the chat. You can then run or edit the workflow by conversation.

### Test with a live data prompt

Try a prompt such as “Show me my worst performing campaigns this month” to see real numbers from your ad accounts. The answer appears as an interactive dashboard you can filter by date range.

## What I'd do

I start each week by opening ChatGPT, confirming I have Plus, then adding Adspirer, HubSpot and Zapier in that order. I spend less than five minutes setting up the three connectors. After that I run a quick audit prompt to check ad performance, pull a contact list for outreach and trigger a follow-up sequence in Zapier. The whole process stays inside one chat window, saving me tabs and reducing context-switching.
