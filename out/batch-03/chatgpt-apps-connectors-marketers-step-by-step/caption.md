Stop *switching tabs* connect your tools inside ChatGPT

→ Ensure you have ChatGPT Plus (Developer Mode).
→ Open Settings → Apps.
→ Search for Adspirer, click Connect and authorise your Google, Meta, Amazon, ChatGPT Ads, LinkedIn and TikTok accounts.
→ Add the HubSpot plugin to access CRM records and trigger workflows.
→ Add the Zapier plugin to describe automations and build them by conversation.
→ Test with a prompt like “Show me my worst performing campaigns this month” to see live data from your ad accounts.

I’ve tested these steps while planning creator campaigns and found each connection takes under two minutes. Using Adspirer I paused a Meta ad set and pulled a Google Ads report without opening another tab.
