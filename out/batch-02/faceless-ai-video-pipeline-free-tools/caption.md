A faceless AI video pipeline in 7 free steps.

No camera. No studio. Just a stack you can copy:

→ Script: free models through one endpoint (FreeLLMAPI or OmniRoute)
→ Voice: Kokoro-82M, an open-weight voice model with Apache-licensed weights
→ B-roll: screen-record the real page with OBS Studio
→ Motion: Remotion, free for individuals and companies of up to 3 people
→ Sound: Freesound, filtered to CC0
→ Captions: transcribe your own voiceover, then fix names by hand
→ Schedule: Buffer's free plan, 3 channels

The step people skip is B-roll. A real screen recording of the actual repo or docs page is far more believable than a stock clip of someone typing.

The honest part: free tiers have caps, licences differ, and open models still make confident mistakes. Every fact still needs a human check.
