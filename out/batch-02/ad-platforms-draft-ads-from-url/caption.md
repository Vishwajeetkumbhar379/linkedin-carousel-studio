Your website URL is now an ad brief.

In one week, three ad platforms showed tools that draft ads from what you already have:

→ Google: Asset Studio turns website URLs and photos into videos, powered by Gemini Omni
→ X: X Lift turns your URL into ad options, and can build the whole campaign, targeting included
→ Meta: Advantage+ creative now turns static ads into video, generally available

So the brief is quietly moving from a document to your landing page.

That's good news if your page is clear. Bad news if it's vague, because the AI drafts from what's there.

How I'd test it without wasting budget:
→ fix the page first: offer, price, proof
→ run AI drafts against your best current ad, one variable at a time
→ change things gradually, as Google itself recommends
→ check every claim before it goes live
→ keep a human sign-off on budget and targeting

My take: a clear brief beats a clever tool. That stays true when the reader is a machine.
