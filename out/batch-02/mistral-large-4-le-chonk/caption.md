Mistral just shipped a 1 trillion parameter model.

It's called Mistral Large 4. Its very official nickname: Le Chonk.

What Mistral says about it:
→ 1 trillion parameters, 49 billion active
→ trained on 3,800 GPUs in Mistral's own European datacentres
→ training data spanning more than 160 languages, including every official EU language
→ strong on spreadsheets, slides and PDFs
→ a European deployment run under European law

Why this caught my eye from Germany: European teams often market in several languages, and where data is processed is a real question here. A strong European option helps with both.

The catch: it's a preview. The weights aren't out yet. Mistral says they'll land by the end of the month, after safety testing.

How I'd try it this week: take one real campaign brief, ask it for a German and a French version, and have a native speaker judge the result before you trust it.
