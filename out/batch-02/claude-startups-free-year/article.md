# Anthropic's Claude Startups programme: a free year of Claude Team, $1,000 in API credits, and how to apply

## What Anthropic announced

On 6 October Anthropic expanded **Claude Startups**, its programme for founders building companies on Claude. TechCrunch reported that it launched as part of Anthropic's SF Tech Week event. Approved startups can get:

- a free year of **Claude Team**, for up to five Premium seats, if the company is new to Team
- a one-time **$1,000 in Claude API credits**, available as soon as you're approved
- the **Claude Startup Stack**: partner discounts and credits worth up to $45,000 combined
- virtual office hours with Anthropic's Applied AI team, help building a Claude Marketplace listing, and events like Founder House and hackathons

## Who can apply

Startups founded in the last five years, or funded in the last two. You don't need venture funding: Anthropic says the programme is open to bootstrapped, pre-seed and venture-backed startups. If a VC in Anthropic's partner network backs you, you can also get up to $100K in additional API credits, claimed through your VC.

## How to apply, step by step

1. **Check the dates.** Founded in the last five years, or funded in the last two.
2. **Set up the basics.** Create a Claude Console account and use a company email that matches your website's domain.
3. **Write a short description.** Anthropic says it reviews every application, so write it like a brief: who it's for, what it does, why Claude.
4. **Apply** on the Claude Startups page.
5. **Claim Team in Console.** Once approved, you get an email linking to the Claude Startups page in Claude Console. Sign in there to claim the Team offer.
6. **Redeem partner offers** in the Console. Examples listed today: ElevenLabs (12 months free plus 33M characters), Gamma (25% off the first year of Gamma Pro annual) and Granola (3 months of Business free, up to 10 seats).

## The fine print

- Team is free only for organisations new to Team.
- The "up to $7,000" figure assumes five Premium seats at $100 per seat per month, billed annually ($6,000), plus the $1,000 credit. Fewer seats means less value.
- Credits apply to the first-party Claude API through Claude Console. They can't be used on AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI or other third-party platforms.
- Partner offers come from independent companies, on their own terms, and may not combine.

## What I'd do

If I ran a small marketing-tech startup, I'd spend the $1,000 on one workflow I can measure before anything flashy. Something like scoring creator shortlists against a brief, or checking every campaign brief for missing information before it goes out. Pick a task you already do every week, so you can compare before and after.

I'd give the five Team seats to the people who write the most: briefs, reports, client emails. That's where a shared AI workspace pays back fastest.
