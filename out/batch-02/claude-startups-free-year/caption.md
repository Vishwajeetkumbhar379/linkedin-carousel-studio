Anthropic is giving startups a free year of Claude.

On 6 October, Anthropic expanded its Claude Startups programme. Approved startups get:

→ a free year of Claude Team, up to five Premium seats (if you're new to Team)
→ $1,000 in Claude API credits, once
→ partner offers worth up to $45,000 in total
→ office hours with Anthropic's Applied AI team

Who qualifies: startups founded in the last five years, or funded in the last two. No VC needed. Bootstrapped and pre-seed teams can apply.

What you need: a Claude Console account, a company email that matches your website's domain, and a short description of what you're building.

The fine print, so nobody's surprised:
→ Team is free only for organisations new to Team
→ credits work on the first-party Claude API, not Bedrock or Vertex
→ the "up to $7,000" headline assumes five seats
