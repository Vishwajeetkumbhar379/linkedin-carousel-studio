# Sources: Anthropic's Claude Startups programme: a free year of Claude Team, $1,000 in API credits, and how to apply

| Claim | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|
| On 6 Oct 2026 Anthropic expanded Claude Startups. Members can get a free year of Claude Team (up to five Premium seats for companies new to Team) and a one-time API credit of $1,000, plus the Claude Startup Stack: partner discounts and credits worth up to $45,000. | Claude blog: We're expanding the Claude Startups program: https://claude.com/blog/were-expanding-the-claude-startups-program-to-help-founders-build | 2026-10-06 |
| Members can book virtual office hours with Anthropic's Applied AI team, get hands-on help building and submitting a Claude Marketplace listing, and access events such as Founder House, hackathons and meetups. | Claude blog: We're expanding the Claude Startups program: https://claude.com/blog/were-expanding-the-claude-startups-program-to-help-founders-build | 2026-10-06 |
| Startups founded in the last five years or funded in the last two are eligible to apply. | Claude blog: We're expanding the Claude Startups program: https://claude.com/blog/were-expanding-the-claude-startups-program-to-help-founders-build | 2026-10-06 |
| The up to $7,000 figure is based on one year of Claude Team with five Premium seats at $100 per seat per month, billed annually ($6,000), plus the one-time $1,000 credit; actual value depends on the number and type of seats. Partner offers may not combine. | Claude blog: We're expanding the Claude Startups program: https://claude.com/blog/were-expanding-the-claude-startups-program-to-help-founders-build | 2026-10-06 |
| To apply you need a Claude Console account, a company email that matches your website's domain, and a short description of what you're building; Anthropic says it reviews every application. The programme is open to bootstrapped, pre-seed and venture-backed startups (page read 7 Oct 2026). | Claude for Startups programme page: https://claude.com/programs/startups | 2026-10-07 |
| After acceptance, members get an email linking to the Claude Startups page in Claude Console, where they claim the Team offer. Only orgs new to Team qualify. Credits apply to the first-party Claude API through Claude Console, not AWS Bedrock or Google Cloud Vertex AI. Founders backed by a VC in Anthropic's partner network can get up to $100K in additional API credits through their VC. | Claude for Startups programme page: https://claude.com/programs/startups | 2026-10-07 |
| Claude Startup Stack offers listed include ElevenLabs (12 months free + 33M characters), Gamma (25% off the first year of Gamma Pro annual) and Granola (3 months of Business free, up to 10 seats); the offers come from independent third-party companies, not Anthropic. | Claude for Startups programme page: https://claude.com/programs/startups | 2026-10-07 |
| TechCrunch reported the expanded programme launched as part of Anthropic's SF Tech Week event, with a free year of Claude Team for up to five premium seats and $1,000 in API credits. | TechCrunch: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/anthropic-gives-startups-a-free-year-of-enterprise-service-and-1000-in-token-credits/ | 2026-10-06 |
