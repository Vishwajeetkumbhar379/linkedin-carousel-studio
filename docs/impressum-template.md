# Impressum for buildwithvvish.com (waiting for Vish)

German law (§ 5 DDG, in force since May 2024) requires an Impressum on business websites, and the newsletter and the partner form already count. Vish fills in the brackets; then Claude adds `impressum.html` to `buildwithvish/src/legal/` and links it in every footer.

Nothing here is invented. **The address must be a real postal address where he can be reached (no P.O. box).** If he doesn't want his home address public, ask about a business address service ("ladungsfähige Anschrift"). It must be a real address that accepts post, not just a mailbox.
