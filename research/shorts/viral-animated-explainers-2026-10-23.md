## Retention rules (merged, most specific wins)

1. **Frame 0 is already busy.** Lead with the most visual moment; the 1 to 3 s cliff loses 30 to 50% of viewers ([aibrify](https://aibrify.com/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-curve-playbook)). No name, no logo intro, intro under 1.2 s ([growcreator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/tech-shorts-retention-tips)).
2. **Hook = specific noun + knowledge gap,** 12 words or fewer, second person ("you"). Templates that fit our niche:
   - `This is what happens the second you [everyday AI action].`
   - `When you [tap X], [N] things happen in under [time].`
   - `[Tool] doesn't [what everyone thinks]. It [surprising truth].`
   - `Why [AI thing everyone uses] [weird behaviour].`
3. **Change the picture every 1.5 to 2.5 s** (under 1.2 s tunes out, over 2.5 s dips). One idea per frame, so the eye never has to choose.
4. **Open loop by 30%, reset at about 60%** ("here's the weird part"), strongest reveal last.
5. **Length 35 to 45 s.** Educational sweet spot is 25 to 40 s; past 45 s holding 80% gets much harder. One file for both apps, always under 60 s.
6. **End on a question, not a CTA,** and make the last words lead straight back into the first line. Loops show as a spike above 100% at second 1.
7. **Comment engine:** the closing question must be a two-sided debate everyone has an opinion on (Zack D's "what-if" and gross-out reactions; ours: "do you say please to ChatGPT?"). Pin a comment that takes a side.
8. **3 to 5 Shorts a week keeps reach steady** (aibrify). Vish's plan starts at 1 a week; we can scale once the format proves itself.

## Our format (locked for the series, see `content/shorts/README.md`)
- 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, -14 LUFS, 35 to 45 s. Same file on YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.
- Faceless. ElevenLabs narrator, calm and steady (Zack D contrast), one take for the whole script.
- Look: Vish's night studio (ink, one amber accent #E7A15A, Instrument Serif + Inter Tight + Geist Mono), one continuous 2.5D world the camera travels through, x-ray cutaways, counters, 3D word-box captions.
- Topics: the inside of everyday AI moments (hitting send, generating an image, an AI agent booking something, why AI lies), always true and sourced.
