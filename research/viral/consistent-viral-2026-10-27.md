# What consistently viral accounts do, and Vish's own touch (27 Oct 2026)

This builds on the studies already in `research/` (Kallaway, Charles Zheng, 100xEngineers, Avi Grondin, Bradford Marais, Greg Isenberg, Jaden Ly, and the 1M+ "starting over" videos). It doesn't redo them. The numbers are public counts read on the dates in those files.

**Instagram view counts need a login, and profile grids no longer load without one** (tested today). So consistency is measured by likes and comments, not views.

## The honest finding: nobody hits 1M every time
Even the best account we studied swings by a factor of 180. @kallawaymarketing's reels on 23 Oct ranged from 298 likes to 54,415 likes. What the "always viral" accounts have in common isn't a hit every time. It's four things:

1. **A format people come back for, run as a series.**
   - In the 1M+ "starting over" set, a sequel posted 1 to 4 days later kept 51 to 57% of the first video's views.
   - A topic switch kept 13 to 19%.
   - Series beat one-offs.
2. **Volume.**
   - They post 5 to 7 times a week.
   - Most posts are average; the hits pay for the rest.
3. **A comment engine.** A keyword plus a specific free thing raises comments per like:
   - @100xengineers "Md": 94,404 comments on 134,657 likes (0.70).
   - @avigrondin "LinkedIn": 2.44.
   - Kallaway's best keyword post: 2.87, against 0.22 to 0.50 on his posts without one.
4. **One look, many topics.**
   - Charles Zheng: the same warm 3D world in every video.
   - Kallaway: the same speaker box and canvas in every video.
   - You know the account before you read the name.

## The craft they share (measured)
- **The hook:**
  - Speech starts at 0.00 s.
  - The wound or the claim comes in the first sentence, or as on-screen text on frame one.
  - A second hit lands between 4 and 8 s.
- **Pace:**
  - Kallaway cuts every 4.4 s (median) with a sound hit every 2 s.
  - 100xEngineers cuts every 2.2 s.
  - Charles Zheng doesn't cut at all (one continuous camera move) but has a sound hit every 3 s.
  - Either way, something new reaches the eye or ear every 1 to 3 s.
- **Captions:**
  - One to four words at a time.
  - One key word in a colour pill.
- **Details:** concrete, checkable details beat feelings. For example "220 companies", "39 clips", "94,000 comments".
- **Cover:** the hook as giant type, plus one face and one image that carries the idea.
- **Endings:** a dated sequel promise ("next week: did it work?") or a keyword, never both stacked.

## What we change from this week
- **Post every day on Instagram.**
  - 2 to 3 reels a week from his footage.
  - Motion reels made from the carousels.
  - The carousels themselves.
- **Every reel gets a cover from one template,** so the grid reads as one brand.
- **Every educational post has a keyword with a live guide.** New keywords (ROAST, LOOP, TRENDS, SKILLS, FREE, INSTA, PITCH, HOOK, COMMENT) need a guide before they go out.
- **Story posts end on a question that's easy to answer**, not a keyword. For example "stupid or brave?", "learn German or go remote?", "what would you fix first?".
- **Sequels within 1 to 4 days whenever a post beats the average.**
