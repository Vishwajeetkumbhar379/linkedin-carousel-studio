Source: Crustdata indexed LinkedIn posts, 7 Sep to 7 Oct 2026, keyword Claude OR ChatGPT OR "AI agent", sorted by comments.
33,646 matching posts; the top 24 read in full (text and engagement only; no author details stored).

## The numbers
- Top 24 posts: 519 to 2,470 comments each. Comments beat reactions on 19 of 24 (e.g. 2,470 comments vs 292 reactions; 1,390 vs 366).
- 20 of 24 are a **free resource behind a comment gate**: "I built X with Claude, I'm giving it away, comment KEYWORD". Several add scarcity ("first 100 comments only").
- All 24 are image posts (one strong visual + long text), not videos or text-only.
- Half are in French: French creators dominate Claude content on LinkedIn; the English niche is less crowded.

## Hook mechanics seen again and again
1. First line = claim with a named tool ("I connected Claude to my WhatsApp", "Bye bye manual SEO").
2. Second line = "Free access 👇" or "Comment X and I'll send it".
3. A structured list of what's inside (arrows, 4 to 15 items, grouped under 3 to 5 headers).
4. A personal proof line ("+500 brands", "15+ countries", "1 year testing").
5. Scarcity close ("first 100 comments").
