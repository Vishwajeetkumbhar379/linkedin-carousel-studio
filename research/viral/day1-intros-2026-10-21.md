## Patterns we use
- Speech at 0.00 s; the wound in the first sentence or frame-1 text; a second hit between 4 and 8 s.
- Premise, then name, inside about 4 s; say "first video" or "Day 1". The name only appears glued to the premise.
- Concrete, checkable details beat feelings. One dry joke on yourself near the end.
- End on a promise of what the account will be; soft, dated sequel promises ("part two tomorrow"). None used a comment keyword.
- First posts look raw (one take, propped phone, at home; 0 to 2.6 cuts per 10 s). Later hits move from the person to a format.
- Post #2 within 1 to 4 days, same story, re-introduce yourself: continuations kept 51 to 57 % of the first post's views, topic switches 13 to 19 %. Sequels can beat the original.
- Instagram plays need a login, so Reels are under-represented here; Trial Reels need a professional account with a follower minimum (200 or 1,000 depending on the source, Oct 2026).
