# Trending audio and faceless hooks: what's true (researched 27 Oct 2026)

Vish, 27 Oct: "I have seen reels where they say if you use trending audio, your reel can get viral... research for that and see."

## What's known
- **No official proof that trending audio boosts reach.** Mosseri's ranking signals, as reported by Buffer: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. On audio he said it helps catch people who browse with sound on, and that it isn't required. Figures like "20 to 40% more reach" come from blogs with no primary source.
- **What a trending song really does:**
  - it puts the reel on that song's audio page, where people browse other reels with the sound;
  - it catches sound-on viewers.

  That's discovery, not a ranking boost.
- **It has to be added inside Instagram.** A song baked into the file isn't linked to its audio page and can be muted for copyright. So the export has his voice and sound effects; the song goes on in the app, under the voice.
- **Account type:** @buildwith.vish is a Creator account (docs/instagram-autodm.md). Creator accounts keep the full licensed library; Business accounts only get royalty-free tracks.
- **How to spot one in the app:**
  - an upward arrow next to the audio name while scrolling Reels;
  - Professional dashboard > Tips and resources > Trending audio.

## Faceless hooks
- Instagram counts viewers who leave in the first 3 seconds ("skip rate", added 2025), per teleprompter.com.
- Common openers:
  - a bold claim;
  - a question;
  - a problem called out;
  - a striking visual.
- Faceless scripts are written in two columns: what's said, and what's on screen. Most numbers on vendor blogs are unverified, so we test our own reels in Insights.
