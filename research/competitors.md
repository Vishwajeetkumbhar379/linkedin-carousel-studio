Method: Crustdata LinkedIn post search, keywords "AI marketing / AI marketers / Claude marketing / AI influencer marketing", posted since 1 Sep 2026, sorted by reactions (the index trails live LinkedIn by ~3 weeks, so this covers September).

## Takeaways for Vish
1. Specific time-saved stories (2 days → 2 hours) outperform tool lists. Vish's open-source tools are exactly this. → backlog: `weekly-report-autopilot`.
2. Numbers in the first line work when they are real and sourced.
3. Short-line rhythm + one question at the end is still the dominant caption shape.
4. Nobody in this sample combined creator-marketing expertise with AI tooling. That's the gap.
