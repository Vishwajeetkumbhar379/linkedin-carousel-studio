**What the numbers say:**
1. **His winners are named frameworks about attention:** storytelling, hooks, scripts, a new format. Each one has an extreme promise ("scientifically impossible to skip", "the ultimate", "the best in the world").
2. **Comments explode when the free thing is a tool, not advice.** A database got 28K comments, a script format with a downloadable resource got 19.5K, @avigrondin's setup guide 49K and @100xengineers' GitHub repo 94K. A Claude skill, a template or a repo beats a PDF of tips.

4. **Recency isn't enough.** His newest posts (AI workflows, 298 likes) did far worse than the framework posts. Topic and promise decide, not the date.

## 2. The script skeleton (Db-zJhiuXP3, 469 words, 118 s, 239 wpm)
1. **Hook with an extreme promise for a boring problem:** "how to get people addicted to your storytelling, even if you talk about super boring topics."
2. **Name it:** "It's called the Illusion of Novelty."
3. **Frame it as one framework, with a count:** "Step 1 … Step 4".
4. **One running example** carries every step: drinking water, the most boring topic ever.
5. **Each step:**
   - its name;
   - what it does, in one line;
   - the example in a before-and-after: "If I say X, I've lost you. If I say Y, all of a sudden…".
6. **The recap:** "Those four steps combine to…".
7. **CTA:** "Comment NOVELTY and I'll send you the full breakdown **and the Claude skill** you can feed your scripts into."

## 3. The editing system (measured)
- **Layout:**
  - **Split frame:** a graphics canvas on top (about 55%); his speaker box at the bottom (a rounded rectangle, about 25%, his room visible).
  - **The rhythm:** it alternates with full-frame talking-head shots for punchlines.
- **Pace:** 20 shots in 118 s, a median of 4.4 s, the first cut at 4.7 s, and 55 sound hits (one every 2 s).
- **Captions:** one white bold word at a time, centred at about 62% height, never boxed.
- **Graphics that move with his words:**
  - **Type-on document card.** A dark frosted card types out the exact sentence he's saying ("New research shows that drinking water at a…"), in sync with his voice.
  - **Split comparison.** The card splits into two: *Old advice* and *New reveal* (italic serif labels). Then it collapses back to one.
  - **Framework map.** A red circle node ("Ultimate Storytelling") is wired to 4 icon pills. Each pill lights up as he names its step, and the map comes back as a progress check between steps.
  - **Step titles:** "1. New Reveal" in bold sans, with italic serif accent words.
  - **The proof section switches canvas** from black to light grid paper: a visible "new chapter".
  - **Highlight:** a red translucent circle lands on the key word ("Professional").
  - **CTA card:** "Comment *novelty*" on paper, with a product card (a purple glow, the Claude skill).
- **Cover:** a bold question title with an italic serif second line and a red underline, plus one symbolic image (a silhouette with a red glowing brain) and a red list card of "boring" examples.

## 4. What Vish adopts (in our palette, with true facts only)

   - a named framework with 3 to 5 steps;

3. **Extreme but true promise words:** "so addictive", "the best free…", "the ultimate". The claim must be backed by the resource or Vish's real experience (850+ deals).
4. **Topics ranked by engagement first** (the rule below), then filtered by Vish's truth: his pillars, his proof, a resource he can really give.

## 5. The "viral first" research rule (now in PLAYBOOK §12 and the vish-studio skill)
1. **Collect candidates.** Gather reels in or next to the niche (AI tools, creators, marketing, remote work), posted in the last 90 days.
2. **Rank them by real engagement:**

   ```
   python scripts/research/ig_fetch.py <codes> --out=<dir> --meta-only
   ```

   This reads likes and comments from the public embed, no login.
3. **Model only the winners:**
   - 50,000+ likes, or 20,000+ comments, or a comments-per-like above 1 with 10,000+ likes;
   - those levels usually mean millions of views (an estimate, because views need a login).
   - When Vish can see real views (logged in), prefer reels with 3 to 4 million.
4. **Take apart the top 3** with `reel_teardown.py`: hook, framework, resource, edit. Write down the pattern, not their words.
5. **Fill the pattern with Vish's true facts,** and say which reference it comes from in the shoot sheet.
