WHAT THEY ALL SHARE
1. The first sentence names the topic and the stakes. No warm-up.
2. A small number or a named framework, so the viewer knows the finish line.
3. A free, specific thing behind one keyword (tool, template, playbook, setup).
4. One recognisable look, every time.
5. Volume: 5 to 7 posts a week, most average, the hits pay for the rest.
