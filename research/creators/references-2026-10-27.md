## What we take
- **Bradford Marais (the closest model for Vish's story):**
  - Opens on name, age and a number ("I'm Brad, I'm 25, 6 months ago... 60,000 followers").
  - Then the problem, then "here's what I did differently", then the turn and the result.
  - A free guide closes it.
  - Visuals:
    - a cut every 1.6 s;
    - the words stay where they are said: one or two words at a time, often tucked behind his head;
    - full-colour flat cards (amber and white) with one word in bold serif;
    - his real desk, the keyboard, a pen;
    - the follower count as a real profile screenshot.
  - Take: the flat colour word cards between talking shots, and the real-proof screenshot.
  - Vish's version:
    - the 220 rejections;
    - the 3,000 euro goal;
    - "this account is my CV".
- **Greg Isenberg (the paper-desk look):**
  - Warm paper background, dark rounded devices (a "writing engine" that prints slop, a card reading "Spend less", a fingerprint card for "your own data", an employee badge stamped "ACCESS REVOKED").
  - Every step is a prop that acts out the idea.
  - Numbered cards (02, 03).
  - Take: this as the Brand-Side carousel and teaching-reel look. Calm, tactile and cheap to make in code.
  - His 3 steps match our voice rules exactly: pick a side, voice, your own data. An honest T8 post can show `voice.md` and SlopMonster as Vish's version, credited as inspired by Greg.
- **Jaden Ly (the month):**
  - 10 ideas for new people, 10 for implementation, 10 for "what it's like to work with you".
  - Each idea is made 3 times in 3 formats (talking head, green screen, carousel).
  - The plan in `content/calendar/2026-11-month-plan.md` is built this way.
  - Visual take:
    - a grid-paper card that drops in behind him;
    - one black-and-white shot for the "but wait" turn;
    - a giant equation on screen ("30 x 3 = 90").
- **Kallaway (the series):**
  1. Pick a series type. His 6: skit universe, progress journey, case study breakdown, recurring educational, numbered countdown, docuseries.
  2. Pick a story structure per episode.
  3. Pick a visual format.
  4. Build the topic x format x layout formula that repeats forever.
  - Visuals:
    - a numbered list card (01 to 04) that reappears with the current step lit;
    - his own speaker box at the bottom;
    - one-word captions.
  - Vish's 4 series come out of these steps (month plan, section 2).
- **Charles Zheng (the 3D):**
  - Already the 70% grammar for HOW-AI-EDIT (PLAYBOOK §4).
  - New from this one:
    - the hook names a real object ("This is a quasar, 8 billion light years away from you");
    - the camera only ever zooms out, revealing the bigger system;
    - a sound hit lands every 1 to 2 s.
- **Sneha (B-roll):**
  - movingimagearchive.com, checked 27 Oct:
    - "a searchable library of archival films... drawn from public-domain collections and is free to reuse", built by Cova;
    - search by describing a shot;
    - each clip downloads with its source film and year.
  - Most clips are 4:3 and old, so they work as framed archive cards (a film frame, a year label), not full-screen 4K.
  - We keep a credit list (film title and year) in each project.
