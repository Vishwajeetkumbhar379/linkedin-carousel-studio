## Editing, animation, visual style
- **@_k.elsie, quarterly recap** (instagram.com/reel/DYwset0R5kP)
  - A gratitude recap told while walking through a beautiful location.
  - Floating framed magazine covers and photos sit beside her as proof; one or two small words of caption at mid-frame.
  - White light-flash transitions.
  - Long takes (median 4.8 s): the location carries it.
  - Rule: proof as floating prints, not lists.
- **@riverisbuilding, OpenFilm launch** (instagram.com/reel/Dep0wuhvizo)
  - Opens on the claim "To never edit a video again, I built…".
  - Shows 3D app icons, then the real editor: each feature lights up on the timeline as it's named ("picks the shots", "adds captions", "B-roll, colour", "motion graphics").
  - Ends on the reveal "this video you just watched? …".
  - Rule: a tool reel shows the real interface and ends on a self-reveal. Directly reusable for "I didn't edit this video".
- **@yourrealtorraelynn, negotiation** (instagram.com/reel/DZWLhE3uEhO)
  - Numbers in the first line ("$45k under asking", "$75k instant equity"), then a villain line ("One of us is lying to you").
  - Big yellow keyword captions, partly behind her.
  - Orange film-burn flashes on the turns; short cuts (median 0.44 s) in the hook.
  - Rule: numbers plus a villain in line one, with flashes only on turns.
- **@thetwofaced_, 3D black-bar effect** (instagram.com/reel/DbqEke7oewm)
  - The object breaks out of the letterbox bars toward the camera (a pop-out depth effect).
  - Tutorial sections on grid paper with cut-out photos, hand-drawn arrows, handwritten labels and red highlight tags.
  - Ends on a "Save this Reel" card.
  - Rule: tutorials look like a notebook (grid, cut-outs, arrows), and the end card asks for the save.

  - Caption structure: introduction (who and why read), struggle (been where they are), rise (break a belief), proof (earn trust), then the offer and a free resource together.
  - His claim: 1,000 comments, 100 asked to work together, 700 got the free resource. This is his own number and unverified.
  - Rule: every caption introduces Vish to strangers.
- **@abigaildaniellla, captions** (instagram.com/reel/Da0hP-vxxL1)
  - Huge single-word captions in the first seconds, then smaller ones.
  - A new location or angle for each sentence; framed inset clips (white-border prints) on a white page.
  - "Comment FONT"; part 1 of a 3-part series, ending with "follow for part 2".
  - Rule: big words early, and a series that makes people follow.

## Method
- **Stiles Dichter, "Build one AI setup from everything you've saved"** (stilesdichter.com/guides/campaign, 2 Oct 2026):
  1. Sort saves by topic.
  2. Put everything in one project as steps and rules, each with its link.
  3. Compare: group by topic, keep the most specific working version with its source, flag contradictions, and write one playbook.
  4. Turn it into one skill per job, plus one master skill that runs them in order.
  5. Re-check weekly.

  Applied: this file covers steps 1 and 2, `PLAYBOOK.md` step 3, `.claude/skills/vish-studio` step 4.
